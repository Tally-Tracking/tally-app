Delete your Tally account

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This page explains how to delete your account and your data in Tally, the bill, debt and savings tracker (Tally - Finance Tracker on Google Play and Tally: Bill & Debt Tracker on the App Store).

You only have a Tally account if you signed in to sync between devices. Without one, your data never leaves your phone: see "No account?" below.

Delete your account in the app

  1. If you use Tally on more than one phone or tablet, sign out on the others first (Settings > Sign Out). Otherwise they upload their copy of your data again the next time they sync.
  2. On the phone you're still signed in on, open Tally and tap the gear icon to open Settings.
  3. In the Data section, tap Delete Account & All Data, then confirm.

This needs an internet connection. It deletes your data from our cloud database straight away, clears it from that phone and signs you out. If Tally can't reach our server, nothing is deleted and the app tells you, so you can try again.

Delete your account by email

If you no longer have the app, or you also want your sign-in record removed (see below), email tallytracking@gmail.com from the email address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my Tally account". We'll delete your account and all of its data within 30 days and reply to confirm. If you signed in with Apple and chose Hide My Email, tell us and we'll help you find your account.

What is deleted, and what is kept

Deleted straight away by Delete Account & All Data

  • Everything you synced: your bills, debts, savings goals, categories, monthly history and settings, from our cloud database (Supabase)
  • Everything Tally stored on that phone

Kept until you email us

  • Your sign-in record: your email address and, if you signed in with Google or Apple, the account details they share with us. The app can't delete this yet, so email us and we'll delete it within 30 days. Until then you could sign in again, but the account would be empty.
  • Usage analytics, only if you left analytics on: events about which screens and features you used, never amounts, names or anything you typed. They are deleted automatically after 12 months. Email us to delete them sooner.
  • Bug reports you emailed us stay in our inbox. Ask us and we'll delete them.

No account?

Without an account, your data is only on your phone. To delete it, open Settings and, in the Data section, tap Clear All Local Data (Android) or Clear Local Data (iPhone). Uninstalling Tally deletes it too.

Questions

Email tallytracking@gmail.com. Our privacy policy explains what Tally stores and why, and our support page answers common questions.
